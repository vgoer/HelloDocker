## Memos笔记

> 一款开源、可自行托管的笔记工具，专为快速记录而打造。原生支持 Markdown，轻量级，完全由您掌控。
>
> [github](https://github.com/usememos/memos)





### 1. 安装

```shell
services:
  memos:
    image: neosmemo/memos:stable
    container_name: memos
    restart: always
    ports:
      - 1007:5230
    volumes:
      - ./data:/var/opt/memos
    logging:
      driver: "json-file"
      options:
        max-size: "10m"
        max-file: "3"
```
